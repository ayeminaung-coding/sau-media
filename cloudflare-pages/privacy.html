Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Information we handle

The service handles account identifiers, platform authorization tokens, video files, captions, scheduling data, and publish status information needed to operate the service.

How information is used

We use this information to store uploads, schedule posts, publish content to platforms you select, maintain job status, and troubleshoot failures.

Storage and sharing

Video files are stored in Cloudflare R2. We send content and required metadata to the social platforms you select. We do not sell your information.

Security and retention

Credentials should be stored securely and access should be limited to authorized operators. Data is retained only as long as needed for the service or as required by law.

Your choices

You may disconnect an account, remove scheduled content, or request deletion of stored data through the service operator.
